Kite Wallah

How it works

Kite Wallah helps you decide when and where to kite using your rider profile (weight, ability, quiver), your spots (safe wind sectors, tide rules), and live forecast data from Open-Meteo.

Plan

Pick riders, a spot, and days. The timeline shows the full-day hourly wind (colour = strength), gusts, wind direction, and cross-hatching when you are outside your tide launch window. Each day is rated GO, Possible, Maybe, Probably not, or Skip, with a wind summary and What to expect on the water (launch, riding feel, tips). Dimmed hours are outside your tide launch window. Gusty days (e.g. 18 kt gusting 37) are described as gusty · not light wind.

Now

Enter or fetch current wind, then get kite/board suggestions from your quiver and a suitability score.

Sessions, Quiver & Riders

Sessions logs real days on the water (wind, kite, spot) and tunes per-kite comfort ranges. Quiver lists your kites and boards (labels, colours, repairs). Riders sets weight and experience level. Spots hold coordinates, safe wind sectors, and tide windows.

AI chat (optional)

Ask advisor on Now and Ask about this plan work without payment using built-in rules. Add your own OpenAI API key under Plan → Options for richer answers (you pay OpenAI directly; key stays in this browser only).
